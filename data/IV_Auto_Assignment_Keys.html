    Document Assistance Services

    Title and document support for your vehicle assignment.

    Included at No Additional Cost

    Lienholder Assistance

    1. Request LOGWe contact the lienholder for the LOG and provide title delivery instructions.
    2. Upload & NotifyWe upload the LOG and email the adjuster a copy.
    3. Receive TitleAfter settlement, we receive the original title, upload it, and notify the adjuster.

    Owner Document Assistance

    1. Contact OwnerWe request the title, Power of Attorney, and any other required documents.
    2. Send & TrackWe send documents by trackable delivery and save the tracking information.
    3. Upload & NotifyWe upload returned documents and notify the adjuster by email.
    Tracking & UpdatesStatus and follow-ups are recorded in the Vehicle Assignment notes.

    Transport Only Service

    This service provides transportation between two external locations, such as a residence, business, or repair facility.

    The vehicle does not go to an IV Auto storage yard. For transport to our yard for salvage processing, use a Standard assignment.

    Maximum Authorized Amount

    IV Auto will pay charges up to the maximum amount you authorize. If charges exceed that amount, an email will be sent to the adjuster requesting approval before additional charges are paid.
